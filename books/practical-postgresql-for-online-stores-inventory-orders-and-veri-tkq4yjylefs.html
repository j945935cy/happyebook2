Practical PostgreSQL for Online Stores: Inventory, Orders, and Verifiable AI Assistance

電子書

Have you ever wondered how to build a reliable e-commerce inventory and order management system from scratch using PostgreSQL? Practical PostgreSQL for Online Stores bridges the gap between raw database syntax and real-world business logic. Using a practical "Daily Coffee Curated Shop" case study, this book guides you step-by-step through designing robust schemas, maintaining inventory invariants (Physical = Reserved + Available), handling purchase orders and goods receipts, processing order price snapshots, managing payments and fulfillment without monolithic status bugs, performing ledger reconciliations, optimizing queries with execution plans, securing database roles, and integrating read-only, verifiable AI store manager assistants. Whether you are a developer, store manager, or database learner, this book equips you with essential engineering principles to build transactional systems that are both trustworthy and auditable.
