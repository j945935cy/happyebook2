Web Development in the AI Era

From a One-Sentence Brief to a Live Website: Choose Tools with AI, Build, Deploy, Maintain, and Add Intelligent Features

A website can begin with a single sentence. Turning that idea into a reliable, maintainable site takes clear requirements, sound tool choices, careful testing, and a plan for what happens after launch. This practical guide walks you through the full process. Learn how websites and APIs work, compare no-code, CMS, AI-assisted, and code-based approaches, and use AI to plan pages, build features, and troubleshoot problems. Follow a running project from its first page through deployment and ongoing maintenance. Then explore how to add AI features with clear limits for data, security, cost, and responsibility. You will learn to: • Turn vague ideas into small tasks with observable acceptance criteria. • Choose tools based on your requirements, constraints, and evidence. • Review AI-generated code instead of trusting a polished-looking result. • Prepare a site for deployment, protect secrets, and test production workflows. • Maintain a live site through monitoring, backups, updates, and recovery plans. • Add AI features while considering privacy, reliability, cost, and user safety. Designed for curious beginners, website owners, independent builders, and developers who want a practical framework for working with AI. Companion prompts, checklists, and exercises are available in the linked GitHub repository.
