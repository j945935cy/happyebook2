Hands-On WSL 2

Build a Linux, Docker, and AI Development Workstation

Turn Windows into a capable Linux development workstation, one tested step at a time. This translated and adapted edition of the Chinese WSL 2 guide walks from installation and terminal basics to a complete development environment, with practical workflows for the points that often take the most troubleshooting: Windows/Linux files, Git and VS Code Remote-WSL, Python and Node.js tools, Docker and Compose, WSL networking, and reliable backup and recovery.nnThen connect the workstation to current AI workflows. Learn the transition from Gemini CLI to Google’s Antigravity CLI, run local models with Ollama, build a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) workflow, and connect local AI services with Open WebUI and n8n. The later projects bring the pieces together with a full-stack web app, containerized services, and an AI-assisted refactoring workspace.nnEach chapter includes hands-on tasks, command references, troubleshooting guidance, review questions, and verification checklists. Version-sensitive instructions should be checked against the linked official documentation for your installed release. This is a translated and edited English edition of the source book, not an independently authored work.
